Conclusion: across 5+ independent submit attempts with verified-correct
credentials, spanning both a 504 (Gateway Timeout) and repeated 502 (Bad
Gateway) responses over a ~7-minute window, the login form never varied its
message — it always rendered the generic "Email or password is incorrect."
alert, identical to what a genuine bad-password attempt produces. The frontend
does not distinguish an infrastructure/gateway failure from an actual
authentication rejection.
